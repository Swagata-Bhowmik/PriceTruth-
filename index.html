Why Price Truth?

Two silent tricks are draining your wallet — one loud, one quiet.

🎭

Fake Discounts Everywhere

E-commerce platforms love a big red badge. But the "original" price is often invented.

₹5,000 → ₹2,500 (50% OFF!)

Reality? This product never sold at ₹5,000. Actual market price: ₹2,600. Real discount: only ₹100 (4%) — not ₹2,500.

📉

Your favorite snacks are quietly shrinking while the price tag stays put.
